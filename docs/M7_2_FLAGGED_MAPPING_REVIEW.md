# M7.2 — Focused Review of Flagged Lesson Mappings

**Status:** Initial source review completed for five candidates; two mapping concerns require curriculum-owner decision  
**Sources inspected:** Authored lesson focus, objectives, explanations, lesson blocks, demonstrations, exercises, retrieval prompts, success criteria, and checkpoints  
**Rule:** Findings below do not change curriculum data. Proposed changes remain recommendations until approved.

## 1. Findings summary

| Lesson | Existing mappings | Evidence-based finding | Recommended disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Year 1 Week 2 — Clear single notes (`y1-w02-lesson`) | `harmony.triads`, `harmony.voice-leading` | Focus is fretting-hand basics, clean tone and relaxed pressure. Worked example is a four-fret ladder; checkpoint is to fret one note close behind the fret and coordinate both hands. No lesson content located that teaches or assesses triads or voice leading. | **Escalate / mapping mismatch.** Both current mappings appear unsupported. No existing catalogue ID clearly represents clean single-note fretting/tone fundamentals. Do not substitute an unrelated skill; curriculum owner should decide whether to remove mappings and whether this is a genuine catalogue gap. |
| Year 1 Week 17 — Build the major scale (`y1-w17-lesson`) | `lead.major-scale`, `lead.pentatonic` | Focus/checkpoint cover the major-scale formula and a C major fragment. Lesson steps explicitly introduce A minor pentatonic (A-C-D-E-G), include a pentatonic question, and ask learners to hear resolution to A. | **Keep both provisionally.** Pentatonic is present in authored teaching and retrieval content, not merely a category association. Consider making both outcomes explicit in the focus/checkpoint so the mapping is visible to learners. |
| Year 1 Week 20 — Play with expression (`y1-w20-lesson`) | `harmony.triads`, `harmony.voice-leading` | Focus/objective cover slides, bends, dynamics and phrasing. Worked example and exercises use a five-note slide phrase; checkpoint asks for one expressive technique in a short phrase. No triad or voice-leading teaching/assessment was located. | **Escalate / mapping mismatch.** Current harmony mappings appear unsupported. `lead.phrasing` and `technique.bends` may be relevant to parts of the lesson, but slides and dynamics are not directly represented as distinct IDs. Curriculum owner should confirm whether to map only the supported existing skills or record a genuine coverage gap. |
| Year 1 Week 31 — Hammer-ons and pull-offs (`y1-w31-lesson`) | `technique.alternate-picking`, `technique.string-crossing`, `technique.legato` | The lesson's focus/checkpoint centre on legato, but its demonstration, lesson blocks, exercise instructions, retrieval prompts and worked examples also explicitly teach down-up alternate picking and controlled two-string crossings. | **Keep all three provisionally.** The broader content supports the three mappings. Consider making the combined focus clearer; do not remove the first two solely because the checkpoint emphasises legato. |
| Year 3 Rock & Blues Week 4 — Legato control (`y3-rock-blues-w04-lesson`) | `technique.alternate-picking`, `technique.string-crossing`, `technique.legato` | Lesson explanation and blocks describe alternate picking and string crossings; demonstration steps, exercise instructions, retrieval prompts and worked examples practise two-string crossings and legato. The capstone checkpoint emphasises legato in a short musical example. | **Keep all three provisionally.** The content contains direct learning evidence for all three, although the checkpoint could better expose the alternate-picking/string-crossing outcomes. |

## 2. Quantified result

- **3 of 5** candidate mapping sets are supported by lesson content, subject to improving outcome visibility.
- **2 of 5** candidate mapping sets contain current skill associations with no supporting lesson evidence (Year 1 Weeks 2 and 20).
- No curriculum mapping has been changed.
- No new skill identifier is proposed for immediate creation. The possible gaps should first be compared against the full 34-skill catalogue and other lessons.

## 3. Content-quality observations

1. Year 1 Week 2 and Week 20 use a generic harmony-oriented explanation key idea despite their fretting-hand and expressive-technique lesson focus. This is a content alignment issue as well as a mapping issue; the lesson explanation should be corrected by the curriculum author.
2. Year 1 Week 17 has two genuine learning outcomes, but the top-level focus and checkpoint make the major-scale outcome much more visible than the pentatonic outcome.
3. Year 1 Week 31 and Year 3 Rock & Blues Week 4 teach multiple related picking/legato capabilities. Their checkpoints are narrower than the supporting lesson content. Consider explicit multi-part success criteria rather than removing valid mappings.

## 4. Decisions requiring curriculum-owner approval

- [ ] Approve removing `harmony.triads` and `harmony.voice-leading` from Year 1 Week 2, or provide lesson evidence that justifies retaining them.
- [ ] Decide whether Year 1 Week 2 exposes an existing skill gap for fretting-hand fundamentals / clean single-note tone.
- [ ] Approve removing `harmony.triads` and `harmony.voice-leading` from Year 1 Week 20.
- [ ] Confirm whether Year 1 Week 20 should map to `lead.phrasing` and/or `technique.bends`; do not imply a dedicated slide/dynamics skill exists in the current catalogue.
- [ ] Confirm focus/checkpoint refinements for Year 1 Week 17, Week 31, and Year 3 Rock & Blues Week 4.

## 5. Next review

Continue the 152-lesson register in curriculum order. Apply the same evidence standard: mappings are retained when the lesson actually teaches, practises, or assesses the skill. A generic objective template or broad category label is not sufficient evidence by itself.
