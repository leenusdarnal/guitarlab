# M7.2 — Representative Curriculum Authoring Pilot

**Status:** Content authored; full CI and curriculum review pending  
**Release:** `curriculum-v1.0.2`  
**Scope:** 12 Year 1 / Year 2 lessons plus the separate four-lesson Year 3 Rock & Blues pilot  
**Stable identifiers:** All lesson IDs and the existing 34 skill IDs are preserved

## 1. Pilot purpose

Test a lesson-authoring standard that makes each lesson's target, musical task, evidence and next practice action specific. The pilot deliberately uses a representative mix of chords, rhythm, fretboard, picking, fingerstyle, scales, pentatonic playing, triads, ear training and performance.

This is a controlled batch, not a mass rewrite of the 152-lesson curriculum.

## 3. Authoring standard applied

Each of the 12 lessons now has:
- Three lesson-specific objectives.
- A concrete checkpoint with an observable action.
- Five task-specific success criteria.
- A task-matched worked example and demonstration sequence.
- Four practice stages with specific instructions and success criteria.
- Targeted common mistakes and corrections.
- Retrieval prompts that ask the learner to recall the concept or task.
- An accessible alternative that preserves the learning goal without requiring one technique or posture.

The general comfort/safety guardrail remains, but it no longer substitutes for task-specific musical criteria.

## 4. Mapping alignment in the pilot

- Retained skill IDs only when the learning objectives and assessment cues now make the mapped capability explicit.
- Removed `lead.major-scale` from Year 1 W23, `harmony.open-chords` from Year 1 W26 and Year 2 W4, and the unrelated pitch/melody/interval tags from Year 2 W30.
- Kept the dual mapping in Year 1 W17 because both major-scale and pentatonic outcomes are now taught and retrieved.
- Kept the four ear-skill mappings in Year 1 W37 because pitch reference, interval movement, rhythm and transcription each have a separate criterion.

The separate mapping decision register documents nine further evidence-backed corrections outside these 12 lessons.

## 5. Assessment and learner-data safeguards

- The 34 skill assessment profiles remain draft with 102 observable criteria.
- No numeric cut scores, automated pass/fail decisions or automatic mastery changes are introduced.
- Practice repetitions and lesson completion do not independently prove mastery.
- The curriculum release is immutable; previous progress and evidence stay attached to their original release.
- New progress uses `curriculum-v1.0.2`; no automatic mastery carry-forward occurs.
- Content remains original or properly licensed; no copyrighted TAB or recordings are reproduced.
